# MG251 Asset and Rights Inventory

This inventory separates assets MG251 may control from rights it may issue and assets it does not own.

## MG251-controlled or potentially controlled assets

Each item requires ownership or license confirmation before production use.

| Asset class | Examples | Current treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand assets | MG251 name, logos, color system, domain presentation | Use existing purple/gold identity; verify legal ownership |
| Website assets | `mg251.xyz`, source code, layouts, databases, analytics | Existing repository remains primary platform |
| Original editorial content | Articles, guides, route notes, parking guidance, interviews | May become public or premium after rights review |
| Original media | Photos, video, livestream recordings, parade replays | Rights inventory required before premium access or sponsor sale |
| Sponsor inventory | Defined placement windows, acknowledgments, features, packages | May be sold only through written deliverable definitions |
| Membership benefits | Alerts, archives, early access, premium coverage, partner benefits | May be issued only when operationally defined |
| Verification authority | MG251’s own platform credential and permission system | Does not create government or third-party endorsement |
| Event workflow | Submission, editorial review, publication, corrections | MG251 retains moderation and publication authority |
| Technical records | Entitlements, placement status, credential status, audit events | May be represented on Sui with minimal public data |

## Rights MG251 may issue in the MVP

### Season Pass rights

- Access to specifically configured MG251 benefits for a defined period.
- Display of membership tier and expiration in the MG251 account.
- Eligibility for configured notifications and premium content.
- Controlled renewal, upgrade, suspension, revocation, expiration, and recovery reassignment.

### Sponsor Placement rights

- Reservation of a specifically identified advertising product and publication window.
- Submission of creative for review.
- Delivery of contracted placement after approval and payment.
- Access to fulfillment evidence and the agreed report.

### Verified Organization rights

- Access to an expedited or role-specific submission workflow.
- Permission to manage the organization profile or event drafts within defined limits.
- Ability to submit corrections and official-source information.
- Access to credential status, notices, and renewal workflow.

### Premium content rights

- Time-limited access to specified content while an entitlement is active.
- No ownership transfer of copyright or permanent guarantee of availability.
- No guarantee against copying by an authorized viewer.

## Assets and rights explicitly not tokenized

- Facebook followers, group members, page likes, or social graphs.
- Instagram followers, YouTube subscribers, Patreon members, or X followers as owned property.
- Third-party social accounts or platform API access.
- Mardi Gras organizations, their members, their emblems, or their intellectual property.
- Parades, routes, public streets, city services, or public property.
- The City of Mobile or any government authority.
- Ownership of MG251, equity, debt, revenue, profits, dividends, or sale proceeds.
- Ownership of photographs, videos, or articles merely because a pass grants access.
- A guaranteed resale market, floor price, appreciation, buyback, yield, or cash redemption.

## Rights verification checklist

Before an asset is used in a paid product, record:

1. Asset title and description.
2. Owner or licensor.
3. Evidence of ownership or license.
4. Permitted uses and channels.
5. Geographic and temporal restrictions.
6. Right to sublicense or include in membership.
7. Right to edit, archive, stream, and redistribute.
8. Sponsor or exclusivity conflicts.
9. Retention and takedown requirements.
10. Responsible MG251 administrator.

## Source-of-truth rule

The legal agreement, terms, invoice, payment record, content license, and verification policy control the business relationship. The Sui object records issuance and lifecycle facts; it does not replace the underlying contract or create rights not stated in the controlling documents.
