# Resource Curation Notes

The public resource directory is meant to replace the public need for Linktree while keeping the website focused on Mobile Mardi Gras visitors.

## Public directory purpose

Publish resources that help visitors:

- watch Mobile Mardi Gras live coverage or previous parade videos;
- follow Mardi Gras - Mobile, AL on public social channels;
- plan downtown parking, transportation, and mobility-friendly access;
- find downtown food and drink stops for parade-day planning;
- find Mardi Gras throws, gear, or directly relevant shopping resources; and
- verify official information through official public sources.

## What not to publish by default

Do not automatically publish every link found in the legacy Linktree cache. Keep general discount, affiliate, subscription, health, food-delivery, or unrelated commerce links out of the public Mardi Gras visitor directory unless they are intentionally approved for the site.

The `data/linktree-links.json` file is a durable import cache. It is not the public directory.

## Contact resources

Email and phone entries should move to a dedicated contact or submission flow instead of appearing as normal visitor resources. That keeps the public directory focused and makes it easier later to add moderation, spam protection, and structured submissions.

## Official-source guardrails

Visitor resources are convenience links. Parade schedules, routes, cancellations, road closures, parking rules, towing, public-safety instructions, and weather impacts should be verified through official City, public-safety, parade organization, and National Weather Service sources.

Weather risk should not be treated as a parade cancellation unless an official source says so.
