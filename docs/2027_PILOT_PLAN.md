# MG251 2027 Pilot Plan

Status: Draft. Participant scope, dates, products, prices, and launch approval remain open.

## Pilot objective

Validate that MG251 can convert audience attention into direct, usable member relationships; provide sponsors with clearly defined and verifiable placements; allow approved organizations to manage accurate information; and use Sui without requiring ordinary users to become cryptocurrency experts.

The pilot is not a token-sale, investment, marketplace, or Mainnet experiment.

## Staged pilot

### Stage 1 — Internal fictional-data rehearsal

Participants: Peter and approved technical testers.

Environment: local/development plus Sui Testnet.

Validate:

- product/status configuration;
- mobile screens;
- role separation;
- fictional issuance and recovery;
- sponsor slot locking and fulfillment;
- credential permission boundaries;
- webhook replay protection;
- gas policy;
- reconciliation and incident procedures.

### Stage 2 — Closed nonpaying account pilot

Participants: small invited group using real accounts only after privacy/authentication approval; no real card charges unless separately approved.

Validate:

- social sign-in completion;
- embedded Sui account reliability;
- sponsored transaction cost/failure rate;
- dashboard comprehension;
- access checks and notification preferences;
- recovery/support workflow;
- mobile accessibility and performance.

### Stage 3 — Sponsor and organization operational pilot

Participants: limited approved local businesses and organizations under written pilot terms.

Validate:

- inventory clarity;
- reservation and approval time;
- creative workflow;
- fulfillment evidence;
- credential application and renewal;
- event revision accuracy;
- support burden;
- editorial control.

### Stage 4 — Controlled 2027 member pilot

Participants: approved limited cohort.

Requirements before entry:

- final membership product and benefits;
- approved terms/privacy/refund policy;
- production-grade auth and database;
- test-mode and then approved production payment configuration;
- security/privacy acceptance gates;
- support and rollback plan;
- no unresolved high-risk reconciliation issue.

## Candidate measures

### Member funnel

- Membership landing-page visits.
- Sign-in start/completion rate.
- Checkout start/completion rate.
- Paid/authorized order to pass-issuance success.
- Median issuance time.
- Gas sponsorship success and cost per successful issuance.
- Dashboard and premium-content access success.
- Renewal/upgrade interest.
- Recovery completion and abandonment.
- Support contacts per 100 members.

### Sponsor operations

- Inventory view-to-request conversion.
- Reservation completion.
- Time from request to confirmed placement.
- Creative approval iterations.
- On-time campaign start.
- Fulfillment evidence completion.
- Reconciliation/dispute count.
- Sponsor satisfaction and repeat intent.
- Administrative time per placement.

### Organization operations

- Application completion.
- Review time.
- Approval/rejection/abandonment rate.
- Accuracy of submitted information.
- Number of editorial corrections.
- Time from submission to publication.
- Representative change/recovery cases.
- Organization satisfaction.
- Administrative time per organization/event.

### Reliability and security

- Authentication failures.
- Sponsored transaction rejection/failure.
- Payment webhook duplicates/retries.
- Duplicate issuance prevented.
- Inventory conflict attempts prevented.
- Entitlement/database mismatches.
- Unauthorized-action tests/alerts.
- Privacy/security incidents.
- Time to detect and resolve exceptions.

## Success thresholds

Peter must approve numerical thresholds in `DECISION_LOG.md`. The pilot should not be declared successful solely because tokens were issued. A useful threshold set should include:

- user comprehension of what the pass represents;
- acceptable mobile completion and support burden;
- reliable entitlement access;
- affordable gas sponsorship;
- no uncontrolled double booking or duplicate issuance;
- sponsor deliverables completed as promised;
- improved event-information accuracy;
- manageable administration time;
- no material privacy/security failure.

## Participant communication

Pilot users must receive plain-language notice that:

- the product is a membership/service record, not an investment;
- Sui operates in the background;
- the applicable environment is a pilot;
- benefits and support boundaries are defined;
- public onchain metadata is minimal but durable;
- copying premium content after authorized access cannot be technically eliminated;
- how to report access, privacy, payment, or accuracy problems.

## Support plan

Define:

- member support channel and hours;
- sponsor/organization escalation;
- account recovery owner;
- payment/refund owner;
- security incident owner;
- editorial correction path;
- expected response targets;
- emergency pause/rollback messaging.

## Rollback and pause criteria

Pause new issuance or affected features for:

- capability/sponsor key compromise;
- uncontrolled duplicate issuance;
- payment reconciliation failure;
- sponsor inventory double booking;
- unauthorized credential/role escalation;
- public exposure of prohibited private data;
- material access-control bypass;
- inability to reconcile authoritative state;
- provider outage creating unsafe behavior.

Public content may remain available while affected membership operations are paused.

## Post-pilot decision

At the end of each stage, document:

1. metrics and data coverage;
2. user/sponsor/organization feedback;
3. security/privacy incidents;
4. administrative workload;
5. financial/gas cost;
6. unresolved defects;
7. policy changes;
8. decision to proceed, revise, pause, or stop.
