# Route A inspiration

The course uses three themed sections: Royal Street & Bienville, Government Street, and Broad & Spring Hill. Green roadside signs mark Royal Street, Bienville Square, Conception Street, Government Street, Spanish Plaza, Washington Avenue, Broad Street, Spring Hill Avenue, and the Civic Center area. A decorative park fountain and a greater concentration of oaks distinguish the Bienville section; civic-style buildings and grandstands emphasize Government Street.

Source: City of Mobile 2027 Route A map (map creation date September 1, 2026), retrieved through MG251's existing `/api/route-maps/a` endpoint. Official source: https://open-government-cityofmobile.hub.arcgis.com/documents/3482a62f130040138c7d0cdc21e1607b/explore

The route and labeled places were visually checked against the city map. Section spacing, terrain, road curves, and the condensed sequence remain fictional for arcade play. The fountain is original decorative artwork, not a surveyed replica. No current Civic Center building is depicted. The garage explains that this is Route A–inspired and links to MG251's official route-map viewer.

Physics, traffic, pickups, timers, and score calculation are unchanged. Parade billboard names and date/time formatting are unchanged; Route A signs are separate scenery placed opposite the nearest parade board. The decorative artwork is generated once at preparation time, not each frame.

Validation: existing game, route, database/replay, and community tests pass. Desktop section captures were inspected using local test-only camera positioning; those camera controls are not shipped.
