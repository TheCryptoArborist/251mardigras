# Business billboards

User-requested boards are separate from parade schedule boards. Lemon T’s and Pop’s Midtown each appear once per stage (six placements total) in both modes. Layout: original logo, company name, website beneath. Images are packaged locally, contained without cropping or recoloring, and the board canvas is prepared once. No race-time external requests or links steal focus.

Sources:
- Lemon T’s: https://www.lemon-ts.com/ — header logo https://www.lemon-ts.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/websitegraphics.png retrieved November 24, 2026.
- Pop’s Midtown: https://popsmidtown.com/ — original logo reused from the website repository’s `public/images/food-stops/pops-midtown.jpg`.

NickNacks Custom 3D Prints appears once in the first stage, at 49% of the section. Its original `NickNacks.jpg` was supplied by the site owner in `NickNacks.zip` on November 24, 2026 and is packaged unchanged. The full confirmed business name appears below the image, followed by its website `www.nicknackscustoms.com`. The website replaces the previous Facebook mark and handle, while retaining the taller logo panel.

These are display placements requested by the site owner; the game does not claim paid sponsorship or endorsement. Physics, score calculations, and the 45 parade records are unchanged.
